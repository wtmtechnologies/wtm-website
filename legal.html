  • Private content is end-to-end encrypted. The private items we relay for you, including friend requests, squad invitations, privately shared pins, your friend connections, and the live location you share, are encrypted on your device using established public-key cryptography (Curve25519/Ed25519 with authenticated symmetric encryption). Our servers store only ciphertext and minimal routing metadata. We cannot read the contents of your private items or your shared location.
  • Public pins are public, by design. A public pin is a broadcast. Its title, description, location, and attached media are visible to all users and to us. Public pins are posted anonymously: they carry no user ID that other users can see. WTM keeps a sealed record of which account uploaded each public photo or video, which staff cannot read; it is opened only to enforce the Terms (strikes, intimate-image and copyright removals), respond to legal process, or meet child-safety reporting duties. For a friends-only or private pin, a sealed record of which account sent its photo or video is kept for about 48 to 72 hours, names no pin, friend, or location, and is opened only if that item is reported as possible child sexual abuse material.
  • Media scanning. Every uploaded photo and video is automatically scanned for illegal or abusive content before it is stored. The media is decrypted only briefly in a secured server function, checked by automated classifiers (see II.5), and, if allowed, stored encrypted at rest. We do not keep or log the plaintext.
  • Text scanning. Text you post publicly is screened before it is published: a pin title, description, and typed address; a business name and description; and announcements. Screening uses local rules plus an automated language model (see II.5). Text our scanner reads out of an image you upload is screened the same way.

WTM ("What's the Move?"): CHILD SAFETY POLICY

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 · Provider: WTM Technologies LLC ("WTM") · Contact: admin@downloadwtm.com

WTM has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and for any sexualization or exploitation of minors. This policy explains what we do about it. It is part of our Terms of Service (Section 8.2).

1. Who can use WTM

WTM is for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to use it. If we learn a minor is using WTM, we close the account. To tell us about a minor, write to admin@downloadwtm.com.

2. Detection

  • Every photo and video is screened before it is published. Pin media can only be taken with the in-app camera at the time of posting, and it is checked by automated systems before anyone else can see it. Nothing that fails the check, or that could not be checked, is published.
  • Reports. Anyone can report a pin, a photo or video, or a profile in the app. A report that says a minor may be involved is never closed without action because a check failed.

3. What happens when apparent CSAM is found

  • It is blocked. It is never published, or it is taken down at once.
  • It is preserved. An encrypted copy and the facts a report needs (when and where it was posted, and which account posted it) are kept in a locked store that no one at WTM can open or view. No one at WTM views, downloads, forwards, or keeps the material.
  • It is reported. We report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) through its CyberTipline, as 18 U.S.C. 2258A requires, and make the preserved copy available to NCMEC and law enforcement on request.
  • The account is suspended. When a case is confirmed (a match against known abuse images, or our second, stricter check at the confidence we report at), the account that posted it is suspended at once. A lower-confidence flag preserves the item but suspends no one, so a false alarm cannot lock someone out. A person at WTM decides whether a suspension becomes a permanent ban (Terms of Service Section 9).
  • Every access is logged. Each time preserved evidence is viewed in our admin console, filed, reminded about, or deleted, the access is recorded in an append-only log.

4. How long evidence is kept

Preserved evidence is kept for the period the law requires (currently one year from detection, or from our report to NCMEC), then deleted automatically. A legal hold or a request from law enforcement can extend that.

5. Non-consensual intimate images

Intimate images shared without consent can be reported in the app or at admin@downloadwtm.com. Valid requests are removed within 48 hours. If the person shown may be a minor, the report is handled under this policy.

6. Contact

Child safety, law enforcement, and NCMEC: admin@downloadwtm.com. If a child is in immediate danger, call 911.
