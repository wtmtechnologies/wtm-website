II.3 How your data is protected, the honest encryption description

  • Private content is end-to-end encrypted. The private items we relay for you, including friend requests, squad invitations, privately shared pins, your friend connections, and the live location you share, are encrypted on your device using established public-key cryptography (Curve25519/Ed25519 with authenticated symmetric encryption).   • Public pins are public, by design. A public pin is a broadcast. Its title, description, coordinates, and attached media are visible to all users and to us. Public pins are posted anonymously, they carry no user ID on them, but their content and location are not private.

To do this, the media is decrypted only transiently in a secured server function, checked by automated classifiers (see II.5), and, if allowed, stored encrypted at rest.   • Text scanning. Text you post publicly is screened before it is published: a pin title, description, and typed address; a business name and description; and announcements. Screening uses local rules plus an automated language model (see II.5). Text our scanner reads out of an image you upload is screened the same way.
