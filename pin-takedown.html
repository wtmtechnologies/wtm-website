Pin Removal

How a pin comes down

A pin is something a user posted about a place. This page explains every way a pin is removed, and how to ask us to remove one. You do not need the app, an account, or a lawyer to make a request.

This page is not an emergency service. Nobody is watching it around the clock. If a party is dangerous, someone is hurt, or you are in danger right now, call 911 (or your local emergency number). Removing a pin does not end an event happening in the physical world.

The fastest way, if you can use the app

These work in seconds and do not wait on us.

It is your pin

Press and hold your own pin on the map, or open your profile and use My Pins. It comes down immediately, for everyone.

You are at the location

Open the pin, then use Flag: Party Over / Unsafe. Enough flags from people actually at the spot pulls the pin automatically, with no wait for a human. The same prompt offers a one tap call to 911.

Anything else

Use the in app Report button on the pin, or email us using the section below. Reports go to automated review and are acted on without waiting for a person.

Every way a pin is removed

Most pins come down on their own. WTM is built so that removal does not depend on any one person being awake.

1. The person who posted it deletes it

Any user can delete their own pin at any time. If someone asks the host to take their party down, this is usually the quickest route.

2. Automated screening, before it is ever public

Photos, videos, and text are scanned automatically before a pin publishes. Prohibited content is rejected and the pin never appears. Screening is automated and is not perfect in either direction.

3. The crowd votes it down

People physically inside the pin's area can flag it. Once enough distinct people flag it, roughly 30% of its raters for a small pin scaling to 10% for a large one, and never fewer than 3 people, the pin is hidden automatically. No staff approval is involved.

4. It expires on its own

A pin that never gets going, fewer than 4 ratings in its first hour, is removed automatically. A pin whose crowd has dropped to about a tenth of its peak is treated as over and removed too.

5. A report is upheld

Reports are reviewed by automated systems that decide to remove, restrict, or dismiss. Suspected child sexual abuse material is quarantined and reported to NCMEC as federal law requires.

6. We remove it

We may remove any pin at any time, at our discretion, including at the request of a resident, property owner, venue, or law enforcement agency. We do not have to agree that we are legally required to, and removing something is not an admission that we were.

After a pin is removed, the spot stays locked. When a pin is deleted or voted down, no new pin can be dropped at that location until the next midday. That stops a removed party from simply being re posted a minute later. Removals are reversible for a short grace period before they become permanent, so a pin taken down by mistake can be restored.

Request removal, without the app

For neighbors, residents, property owners, venue managers, schools, and law enforcement. Email us and we will act on it.

admin@downloadwtm.com

Tell us where

The street address, or a screenshot of the pin, or its coordinates. Location is the one thing we cannot work without. Pins are posted anonymously, so we identify them by place, not by who posted them.

Tell us why

For example: it is your home, you did not consent, it is on private property, it is a school, it is unsafe, or the content itself breaks the rules. A sentence is enough.

Tell us who you are

Your relationship to the place and a way to reach you. You do not need to be a WTM user. Law enforcement: include your agency, name, badge or ID number, a callback number, and say plainly whether this is an emergency.

Please be realistic about timing. WTM is a small product, not a staffed 24 hour operation. Email is not monitored continuously. If something needs to stop right now, call 911, and use the in app flag if you are at the location, because that removes a pin automatically without waiting for us.

Categories we treat as urgent

Intimate images shared without consent

Reported non consensual intimate imagery is removed within 48 hours, as the law requires. If the person depicted is a minor, it is handled under our child safety process and reported to NCMEC. Email admin@downloadwtm.com or use the in app report option.

Child sexual abuse material

Quarantined immediately and reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children under 18 U.S.C. 2258A. This is the one review a person performs, and only to make the report the law requires.

Copyright

Send a DMCA notice to admin@downloadwtm.com. Include the work, the material to be removed, your contact details, and the statements the statute requires. See the Terms.

For law enforcement: what we can actually produce. We respond to valid legal process, but by design WTM holds very little. Pins are posted anonymously and carry no user identifier. We do not have real names. Phone numbers are kept only as a one way hash, never in readable form. Private items between users are end to end encrypted and we cannot read them. What we can generally produce is an anonymous account identifier, account creation time, and a public pin's already public coordinates and timestamps. In a good faith emergency involving a risk of death or serious physical injury, we may voluntarily disclose the limited information we hold, as the law permits. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.
